01 Profile

About Juned Ghanchi

Juned Ghanchi is the CEO and Co-Founder of Optimize Tech Studio, a software development and AI engineering company serving businesses across international markets. With more than 10 years of experience in software, mobile technology, digital products, and technology delivery, he works closely with startups, SMEs, and established businesses on software planning, product development, AI adoption, and digital transformation.

His experience spans custom software development, mobile app development, SaaS products, AI solutions, enterprise applications, software modernization, outsourcing, and product strategy. He also works with development teams on project estimation, technology selection, delivery planning, scalability, and long-term software decisions.

Juned writes about software development, artificial intelligence, mobile applications, SaaS, development costs, outsourcing, product engineering, and emerging technology trends. His articles combine practical business experience with insights from working with software teams and clients across different industries and markets.
