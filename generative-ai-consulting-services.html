Our Generative AI Consulting Services

Optimize Tech Studio provides AI consulting services from its Buffalo, New York presence, helping enterprises evaluate and implement generative AI opportunities with practical, model-independent guidance. Our generative AI consulting connects business goals with use-case prioritization, technology assessment, data readiness, risk evaluation, architecture, and implementation planning, with hands-on support available as initiatives move toward implementation.

  1. Gen AI Strategy Development

    Our GenAI consultants develop enterprise AI strategy around business objectives before technology choices begin. We connect investment priorities with an AI operating model, AI portfolio, and governance needs. A 12–18 month AI capability roadmap then sequences initiatives around business value, ownership, and execution.

  2. AI Use Case Discovery and Prioritization

    We identify, assess, and prioritize enterprise generative AI use cases by mapping workflow pain points against business value and implementation effort. Our team assesses 10–20 use cases through value-feasibility scoring, creating a priority shortlist before teams commit budget or data.

  3. Technology Assessment

    Our AI technology selection compares large language models, vector databases, model APIs, and orchestration frameworks against workload needs. We assess 3–5 technology options for architecture fit, inference cost, security, latency, and deployment requirements before making build-versus-buy recommendations for use cases.

  4. Custom Development and Integration

    Our GenAI engineers integrate AI applications with business systems through APIs, function calling, and workflow orchestration. We apply 20+ integration patterns with human-controlled workflows, mapping role permissions, approval logic, and API limits before automating operational actions across applications.

  5. Risk and Compliance

    High-impact AI use cases require controls before deployment, not after release. Our consultants assess generative AI risks against NIST AI RMF, OWASP guidance, data privacy requirements, and prompt-injection threats. We define human oversight, output validation, access controls, and approval checkpoints for high-impact workflows before deployment.

  6. Gen AI Integration

    Our generative AI integration services connect Retrieval-Augmented Generation, agent orchestration, model gateways, and enterprise APIs with CRM, ERP, knowledge bases, and SaaS platforms. We use permission-aware data access and model-provider abstraction so AI workflows respect user roles and system boundaries.

Our Generative AI Consulting Process

Our user-friendly AI consulting lifecycle moves from discovery and workflow analysis into AI feasibility assessment, Generative AI architecture, and implementation planning. This decision-gated process uses evidence-led validation and business-to-architecture traceability to produce a practical GenAI implementation roadmap with implementation-ready outputs clearly.

1

Business Discovery

AI consulting discovery starts with stakeholder workshops around business objectives, pain points, and existing systems. We target 2–3 days for discovery. The output is a shared problem definition that links stakeholders, operating processes, approval paths, and business goals before AI selection begins.

2

Workflow Analysis

Manual tasks, handoffs, and decision points often hide where AI can actually help. Through process mapping and task decomposition, we trace 20+ workflow steps and information flows. This exposes bottlenecks, workflow dependencies, and realistic automation opportunities without breaking existing approval logic.

3

Use-Case Identification

Once workflow friction is clear, we convert it into AI opportunity identification. AI assistants, RAG systems, AI agents, and document intelligence are matched to knowledge retrieval, content generation, document processing, or workflow automation. The result is 10–20 candidate use cases linked to outcomes.

4

Use-Case Prioritization

We prioritize AI opportunities with a value-feasibility matrix that compares business value, implementation effort, risk, and time to value. Impact scoring, complexity scoring, and dependency mapping narrow the list to the top 3–5 opportunities and guide sensible investment sequencing clearly.

5

Data Readiness Assessment

A GenAI data assessment checks whether each use case has reliable information behind it. We review data quality, data lineage, document quality, permissions, and access controls, targeting 90%+ usable data while identifying gaps that could block retrieval, automation, or outputs.

6

Technical Feasibility Assessment

AI solution validation tests whether a prioritized use case can work within real technical and commercial limits. Over a 2–4 week validation target, we assess model fit, retrieval quality, integration feasibility, latency, accuracy, inference cost, and constraints before recommending investment.

7

Model, RAG, Agent, or Copilot Strategy

The right AI pattern depends on what the use case actually requires. Our RAG strategy compares 3–4 options across retrieval, grounding, tool calling, orchestration, and model routing. We then select RAG, AI copilots, or agentic workflows that fit the architecture.

8

Generative AI Architecture

Production GenAI architecture connects model APIs, a vector database, retrieval pipelines, agent orchestration, guardrails, and enterprise integrations around one operating design. We use an LLM gateway, observability, model-provider abstraction, and security-by-design to support modular deployment with a 99.9% availability target.

9

PoC or MVP Planning

A promising architecture still needs evidence before larger investment. We define the Proof of Concept or AI MVP around an evaluation dataset, acceptance criteria, pilot users, and success metrics. A 4–8 week target keeps validation controlled and tied to measurable outcomes.

10

Implementation Roadmap

The GenAI deployment plan converts validated decisions into a 90-day initial delivery roadmap. We organize the technical backlog, dependency plan, ownership, governance milestones, and rollout phases so each work package has clear responsibility, sequencing, risks, and a path into implementation.

Frequently asked questions.

What does a generative AI consultant do?

A generative AI consultant helps businesses identify where GenAI can create practical value and determine how to implement it. This typically includes AI use-case discovery and prioritization, data-readiness assessment, technical feasibility analysis, model and technology evaluation, RAG or AI agent strategy, risk and governance planning, architecture design, and implementation roadmapping. The goal is to help teams decide what to build, which AI approach fits the use case, what data and integrations are required, and how to move from an AI idea to implementation.

What is included in a generative AI consulting engagement?

A generative AI consulting engagement can include business discovery, AI use-case identification and prioritization, data-readiness assessment, technical feasibility analysis, model and technology evaluation, RAG or AI agent strategy, architecture planning, risk and governance requirements, and an implementation roadmap. Depending on the scope, the engagement may also define PoC or MVP requirements, success metrics, integrations, ownership, and implementation dependencies.

How much does generative AI consulting cost in the USA?

The cost of generative AI consulting in the USA depends on the scope of the engagement, number and complexity of use cases, data readiness, technical validation requirements, enterprise integrations, security requirements, and whether a PoC or MVP is included. Optimize Tech Studio scopes consulting engagements around the business problem and required deliverables before providing a project estimate.

How long does a generative AI consulting engagement take?

The timeline depends on the scope and technical complexity. At Optimize Tech Studio, initial business discovery typically has a 2–3 day target, while technical feasibility validation can target 2–4 weeks. If the engagement includes a PoC or AI MVP, the validation and build phase can target 4–8 weeks, depending on data availability, integrations, model evaluation, and acceptance criteria.

How do you identify the best GenAI use cases for a business?

We start by examining business objectives, workflow pain points, manual tasks, information flows, decision points, available data, and existing systems. Potential GenAI use cases are then assessed against business value, technical feasibility, implementation effort, risk, dependencies, and time to value. Our process can assess 10–20 candidate uses cases and prioritize the strongest opportunities for further validation.

How do you choose between RAG, AI agents, copilots, and fine-tuning?

The choice depends on what the use case needs to accomplish. RAG can be appropriate when responses need to be grounded in enterprise knowledge; copilots can assist users within existing workflows; and AI agents can be considered when a use case requires controlled multi-step actions or tool interaction. Fine-tuning can be evaluated when model behavior needs specialized adaptation that prompting or retrieval alone does not adequately provide. We compare these approaches against accuracy, data, security, integration, latency, cost, and operational requirements before recommending an architecture.

How do you assess whether company data is ready for generative AI?

Our GenAI data-readiness assessment reviews data quality, availability, document quality, data lineage, permissions, access controls, and suitability for the intended use case. We also identify missing, inaccessible, outdated, or poorly structured information that could affect retrieval quality, model outputs, or automation before moving into implementation.

Can you integrate generative AI with existing CRM, ERP, and enterprise systems?

Yes. We design GenAI integrations that connect AI applications, RAG systems, copilots, and agentic workflows with CRM, ERP, knowledge bases, SaaS platforms, and other enterprise systems through APIs and appropriate integration patterns. The architecture can incorporate role-based permissions, approval logic, data-access controls, and human oversight so AI workflows operate within existing system boundaries.

How do you address security and data privacy in enterprise GenAI?

We consider security and privacy during architecture and implementation planning rather than treating them as post-deployment requirements. Our assessments cover data access and permissions, privacy requirements, prompt-injection risks, output validation, human oversight, API security, and relevant controls informed by frameworks and guidance such as NIST AI RMF and OWASP. The specific controls depend on the use case, data sensitivity, deployment environment, and applicable organizational requirements.

Can you implement the AI solution after the consulting engagement?

Yes. Optimize Tech Studio can continue from consulting into PoC or MVP development, generative AI application development, RAG implementation, AI agent and copilot development, enterprise integration, testing, deployment, and production engineering. This allows validated consulting decisions and architecture requirements to move into implementation with continuity between the consulting and engineering teams.
