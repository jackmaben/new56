01

Inventory State Management

We separate allocated stock, quarantined stock, damaged stock, and available inventory through inventory state tracking. Configurable stock-status rules control stock status management, while real-time inventory state updates maintain separate available and reserved quantities for accurate fulfilment and allocation decisions daily.

02

Real-Time Stock Visibility

Our live inventory view shows SKU quantities and bin locations through a centralized warehouse dashboard. Bin-level quantity tracking and sub-minute inventory updates keep stock positions current. 03

Inventory Reconciliation

Warehouse stock reconciliation compares physical stock with system stock at SKU and bin level. Automated variance calculation highlights physical versus system inventory gaps quickly. This SKU and bin-level reconciliation process supports 95% faster discrepancy identification before stock adjustments are approved.

Warehouse Exception Tracking

A short pick, damaged stock, or missing inventory needs clear ownership. Real-time exception alerts identify the issue. Automated escalation rules assign responsibility. Our warehouse exception management process uses assigned issue ownership to support 50% faster exception resolution across warehouse workflows.
